You looked at the camel. Abu Hamdan saw you look at the camel, and now he wants 20 rials for it.

The camel is handmade, he says. By hand. My hands. Okay, my cousin's hands. Okay, a factory's hands, but a factory with a lot of heart. It says *made in Yiwu* on the bottom.

## How to play

Type an offer, or just talk. You can compliment him, insult the camel, mention that your cousin sells them cheaper, make a final offer, or walk away, which only works if you mean it. His price moves, your blood pressure moves, and he keeps reminding you how many children he has to feed. The number changes.

It has sound, and you will regret turning it on.
