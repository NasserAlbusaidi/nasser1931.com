*Ard* (أرض) means earth. You start with a young world and a few small lights in its shallow seas.

Under the hood, a climate grid with biomes, seasons, and droughts carries up to 4,200 creatures, each with ten genes: size, speed, senses, diet, habitat, warmth, colour, intellect, sociality, and fecundity. Species split when their genomes drift apart. Life crawls onto land, hunters appear, and cleverness evolves without being told to.

## Then it gets weird

Once a species is clever and social enough, it *awakens* into tribes, and the tribes notice you. Your miracles are read through each tribe's culture: some fear you, some get zealous, some go to war over what you meant. They name you, write laws, split into sects behind prophets, build temples, and make sacrifices. If you go quiet for long enough, they stop believing.

The scripture is generated from things that actually happened in your world, and their creation myth comes from the real family tree of life on the planet. Belief is also your power supply, so you need them to need you.

It runs on raw WebGL2 and raw Web Audio, with no libraries, in one file.
