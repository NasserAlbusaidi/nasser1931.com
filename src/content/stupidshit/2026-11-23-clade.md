You get an island, a pile of genes, and god powers. Every animal carries 37 genes, two copies each: size, speed, senses, diet, camouflage, toxins, courtship, parental care, and more. They breed, mutate, and drift apart, and when two populations drift far enough they stop being the same species.

## The bit I like

The bodies come from the genes. Each species gets a mesh sculpted from its genome, so legs, wings, fins, crests, and eyes appear because evolution put them there. When a species drifts, its body gets rebuilt. Skin patterns (scales, fur, plates, stripes, spots) come from genes too.

Behaviour is where the arms races happen. Predators stalk and sprint, and the faster ones win chases. Prey evolve camouflage that matches the ground and alarm calls. Some prey turn toxic, and predators learn to avoid those colours, which means mimics can show up and free-ride on the warning. Males display, females choose, and mothers defend their young.

## God tools

Spawn, yeet, smite, meteor, radiation, a love zone, a monolith, plague, and climate events. The Population tab charts the arms races over time.

Predator and prey numbers boom and bust, and every seed plays out differently. Sometimes the predators die out. That's nature, and also partly my tuning.
