Rub the lamp and you get three wishes and a jinn with opinions.

> "Four thousand years. Nobody. Ever. Asked that. They all want money."

Wish for money and you'll get it, in the most technically correct way possible. Ask for more wishes and he has been waiting for you since the Bronze Age. Poke him in the face and he'll remember it the next time you rub the lamp.

It's short, it's rude, and you will try the "more wishes" one. Everyone does.
