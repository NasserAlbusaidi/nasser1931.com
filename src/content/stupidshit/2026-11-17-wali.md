This started as **[Hara](/play/hara)**, a slider toy: one small town in the interior, with sliders for heat, rain, goats, petrol price, AC thermostat, shawarma stands, *wasta*, and speed bumps. You push sliders, something breaks, and a log called "What the fuck just happened" explains it to you. Turn the AC down to 16 and see what the grid thinks of that.

It turned into **Wali II**, which is a real game.

## Wali II

Muscat has handed you Hara: 285 people, one generator, two falaj channels, a ruined fort full of goats, and a wadi that floods. You get three terms of four years, and you need 50% at every election.

- **Build** roads, houses, farms, falaj channels, solar, and dams on a tile map. Everything needs a road link to the highway.
- **Power and water**: summer heat doubles power demand, and the aquifer only goes down unless it rains.
- **Four factions** (elders, youth, merchants, farmers) want different things, and edicts cost *wasta*.
- **Muscat** sends money once a year, and directives whenever it likes. Your cousin Hamad is always nearby.

Bots that do nothing or click at random lose in the first term every time. A decent heuristic bot wins its first term about three times in four, then dies in the second or third. Good luck.
