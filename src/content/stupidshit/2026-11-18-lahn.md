*Lahn* (لحن) means melody. Every sound in it is synthesised in the browser with Web Audio. There are no samples.

Six cues, each an original melody written in a genre:

- **Arrakis**: desert epic in D Phrygian dominant, with a throat drone and a voice lead.
- **Hyperspace**: a C Lydian brass fanfare.
- **Tick**: a Shepard tone and a clock ostinato that never quite resolves.
- **Event Horizon**: organ.
- **High Noon**: a whistled western with plucked guitar.
- **Rain City**: CS-80 style noir.

## Modes

- **Learn**: notes fall towards a keyboard over two passes, and you get graded S to F, with commentary you may not enjoy.
- **Free jam**: pads locked to the cue's scale, chords on Shift, four FX buttons.
- **Looper**: stack 8-bar layers.
- **Take**: record yourself, then export the take as a WAV.

You can also drop in your own MIDI file, pick a hand, and learn it on a piano keyboard. Use headphones: half the point is the sub-bass.
