A young world, a few small lights in its shallow seas, and you.

They will multiply, split into kinds, crawl ashore, eat each other, and one day one of them will look up. After that, everything you do becomes scripture.

Your power is their belief. Answer prayers, or don't. Rain, bounty, fire, plague, stars. They will remember all of it, and they will remember it wrong.
